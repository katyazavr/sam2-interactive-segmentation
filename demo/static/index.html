04 / METHODOLOGY

Controlled by design.

Exactly one major experimental change: the prompt.

Pretrained SAM 2.1

Hiera Base+ weights, no training or fine-tuning. Best-scoring mask from three candidates.

Image-level split

COCO 2017 val split into 60/20/20 development, validation and test. Seed 42; no overlapping images.

Controlled prompts

Point from the GT mask's distance-transform center. Box from the GT annotation (oracle).

Paired evaluation

Both methods scored on the same object instances. IoU, Dice, latency and failure analysis.

Important limitation. Oracle COCO bounding boxes are exact. Boxes drawn by people or predicted by detectors may give lower performance. These results do not evaluate a separate object detector.
